# WAEC Exam Prep

A practice-test product for Ghanaian students preparing for WAEC exams, covering both Junior High (BECE) and Senior High (WASSCE) tracks in one app.

## Language

### Exams

**WAEC**:
The West African Examinations Council, which sets and administers BECE and WASSCE in Ghana.
_Avoid_: exam board, the council

**BECE**:
Basic Education Certificate Examination, taken at the end of Junior High School.
_Avoid_: JHS exam

**WASSCE**:
West African Senior School Certificate Examination, taken at the end of Senior High School.
_Avoid_: SHS exam, SSCE

**JHS**:
Junior High School, the BECE track.

**SHS**:
Senior High School, the WASSCE track.

**Exam Toggle**:
The in-app switch that moves a student between the BECE (JHS) and WASSCE (SHS) tracks.

### Content

**Past Question**:
A question previously used in a WAEC exam, used as source material for the bank.
_Avoid_: real question, official question

**Variation**:
A practice item derived from a past question, with numbers changed and wording lightly adjusted.
_Avoid_: clone, copy, remix

**Practice Set**:
A short run of questions (target: 10) with instant per-question feedback. The product's core unit.
_Avoid_: quiz, test

**Lesson**:
Instructional content mapping the GES syllabus to past-question patterns.

**Topic**:
A subject subdivision grouping related questions, e.g. Mathematics to Fractions, Addition, Factorization.
_Avoid_: category, chapter, unit

**Explanation**:
A one-line note on why the correct answer is correct, attached to each question and linked to the Lesson covering its Topic.

**Question Type**:
The form of an item; v1 ships multiple-choice questions only.
_Avoid_: format, kind

### Product

**Practice Loop**:
The core flow: pick exam, pick subject, answer a practice set, see what was missed.

**Progress Tracking**:
The thin slice of streak and per-subject accuracy shown to a student.

**Anonymous Mode**:
Using the app with no account; progress is kept on-device.
_Avoid_: guest mode, trial

**Free Allowance**:
The number of questions a student may answer per subject before the sign-in wall; currently 10.

**Sign-in Wall**:
The point where a student who has exhausted their free allowance must sign in to continue.
_Avoid_: paywall, login gate

**Exam Mode**:
An optional timer toggle that makes a practice set behave like the real timed exam.
_Avoid_: timed mode, test mode

**Free Tier**:
The ad-supported access level that keeps the practice loop genuinely useful.

**Paid Tier**:
The subscription level unlocking lessons, unlimited practice, analytics, and offline bank download.
_Avoid_: premium, pro

**Term Pass**:
A paid tier priced per school term rather than monthly.

### Presentation

**Track Theme**:
The visual mode that follows the Exam Toggle: light for BECE (JHS), dark for WASSCE (SHS). It is derived from the track, not a separate user setting.
_Avoid_: dark mode, appearance setting, colour scheme
