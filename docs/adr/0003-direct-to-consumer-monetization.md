# Direct-to-consumer monetization, no school channel

We sell directly to students and parents through a freemium model (free tier with rewarded ads shown only when online; paid tier at GH₵10–15/month or roughly GH₵40 per school term), rather than licensing to schools. Schools were a genuine distribution alternative, but we chose direct reach and a self-serve mobile-money funnel to keep the product independent of institutional sales cycles.

**Status**: accepted

## Considered Options

- B2B per-student school licenses (slower sales cycle, institutional dependency, but easier bulk distribution).
- Direct-to-consumer freemium with mobile-money micro-payments (chosen).

## Consequences

- No institutional sales function is needed; growth depends on consumer acquisition (sharing, web/app discovery).
- Payments must be consumer-grade mobile money (Paystack), not invoiced billing.
- Ads are rewarded-only and online-only, so offline free users generate no ad revenue by design.
