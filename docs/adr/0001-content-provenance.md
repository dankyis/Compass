# Content provenance and legal posture

We ship WAEC past questions as the backend question bank, applying number variation and only light wording changes; some items ship verbatim. We accepted this posture deliberately to reach a usable product fast, knowing WAEC asserts copyright and Kingdom Books holds Ghana sole publishing rights, so the bank is legally exposed.

**Status**: accepted

## Considered Options

- License content from WAEC / Kingdom Books (low risk, cost + negotiation).
- Ship only fully original, curriculum-aligned items, using past questions privately as a topic/difficulty blueprint (lowest legal risk, slowest to author).
- Ship past questions with number variation and minimal rewording (chosen).

## Consequences

- The item bank is a derivative of WAEC material and carries takedown / licensing risk; this may need to be revisited before scaling or raising money.
- Future engineers should not "fix" the light-edit approach without knowing it was a deliberate speed-over-safety trade-off.
